BEFORE vs AFTER - CFA / DEMOSAICING ENGINE
==========================================

PART 1 - THE SIX SUPPLIED IMAGES
Every image is JPEG at estimated QF74. The SKILL calls JPEG below ~90-95%
"the dominant, universally-reported failure mode" and the engine's gate skips
at or below QF85. All six are skipped BEFORE and AFTER. This is correct, and
it means the supplied corpus cannot measure this engine at all.

demosaicing it; the forgery is a 128x128 region demosaiced at a mismatched
phase. This is the only place the enhancements are visible.

HONEST RESIDUAL DEFECT
  Coherence filtering reduces the high-quality false positive but does not
  remove it. Across 6 authentic scenes the worst case at QF100 is still
  0.9912. An earlier 3-scene run showed 0.0000 and that was chance.
