FILES AND METHODS CHANGED
  1. computer.py  GradientMagnitudeComputer.compute
     constants.py DEGENERATE_IMAGE_RAW_SCORE
     Risk: none to the contract. It only replaces a number the engine had
     already declared meaningless with one that reads as meaningless.
  2. scorer.py    LightingScorer.to_probability, new UNCALIBRATED_ABSTENTION_NOTE
     engine.py    JpegEngine-equivalent _score_computation gains a may_vote term
     constants.py ABSTAIN_WHEN_UNCALIBRATED
     Risk: the engine now abstains by default, so a fusion layer expecting a
     vote gets none. That is the intent, and it is reversible by supplying
     calibration data - verified: with 40 reference ratios the same image
     returns probability 0.4250 at is_reliable=True.
  3. computer.py  new GradientMagnitudeComputer._interior
     constants.py GRADIENT_BORDER_EXCLUSION_WIDTH,
                  MINIMUM_SIDE_FOR_BORDER_EXCLUSION
     Risk: images smaller than 3 pixels on a side have no interior; the helper
     returns the map unchanged in that case rather than emptying it.

CATEGORIES COVERED
  A (signal extraction) : #3 only. Nothing more was justified - see the
                          rejected list; no reduction of this map responds to
                          lighting at all.
  B (condition checker) : #1 and #2.
  C (scoring)           : #2.
  D (robustness)        : #1 and #3.
  E (performance)       : not needed. 169 ms per image.

TESTED AND REJECTED (do not re-propose)
  - Replacing max_grad with a high percentile or with mean/std. Measured on
    Lambertian ground truth where one object is re-lit from a different
    direction and NOTHING else changes, sensitivity to that genuine
    inconsistency against sensitivity to transforms that change no lighting:
        max/median     0.000000  vs 14.96
        p99.99/median  0.134190  vs 14.53
        p99.9/median   0.887615  vs 13.20
        p99/median     0.004327  vs  5.27
        mean/median    0.000128  vs  0.204
        std/median     0.003716  vs  1.047
    The best nuisance-to-lighting ratio of any candidate is 13.8x. No reduction
    of this map detects lighting, so a percentile would buy stability without
    buying validity - at the cost of changing a formula the SKILL prints
    verbatim as max_grad = max(gradient_mag(:)). Rejected.
  - Re-fitting PROVISIONAL_SIGMOID_MIDPOINT to the observed operating range.
    That would turn a constant into a curve fitted to six unlabelled corpus
    images and one synthetic scene - inventing the calibration the SKILL states
    does not exist ("no normalization or calibration given"). Abstaining is the
    honest response to an absent calibration; guessing one is not.
  - Adding a block-wise or region-wise variant to give the engine spatial
    sensitivity. That is Pipeline B in disguise, and the SKILL tags every step
    of Pipeline B "(not specified in the corpus)" and calls it "entirely
    engineering synthesis". Building it would mean inventing a technique.
