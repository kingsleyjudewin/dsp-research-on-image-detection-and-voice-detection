HOW THE RADIUS IN ROW 4 WAS CHOSEN (matched-scale ground truth)
  Three authentic photographs downscaled to a 256 px long side, and the same
  three with a 64x64 region copy-moved inside them - so authentic and forged
  differ in exactly one thing. Worst-case authentic raw_score against the
  three forged raw_scores:

    radius   AUTH worst    exact copy                blur3            jpeg q90
     0.05      0.0000     0.0256/0.0256/0.0256   0.0000/0.0000/0.0000   .../0.0017
     0.10      0.0000     0.0256/0.0256/0.0256   0.0000/0.0000/0.0000   .../0.0256
     0.20      0.0000     0.0260/0.0267/0.0256   0.0000/0.0000/0.0000   .../0.0256
     0.30      0.0000     0.0262/0.0271/0.0256   0.0000/0.0000/0.0013   .../0.0256
     0.40      0.0003     0.0273/0.0286/0.0256   0.0000/0.0000/0.0116   .../0.0256
     0.50      0.0203     0.0280/0.0302/0.0256   0.0020/0.0017/0.0197   .../0.0256

  0.30 is the largest radius at which all three authentic images still score
  EXACTLY 0.0000. It additionally recovers a JPEG-q90 paste (0.0256) and a
  blurred paste (0.0013) that the shipped configuration missed outright.
  0.50 is rejected: authentic 0.0203 overlaps the forged range, so the two
  classes stop being separable.

RISK CARRIED
  ENHANCEMENT 1 changes what Pipeline A sees at the right and bottom edges of
  a non-conforming image. Symmetric padding is the SKILL's own named choice
  precisely because it avoids introducing spurious high-frequency energy
  there, and the residual is cropped back so the noise-analysis engine
  downstream receives the original geometry - verified on 1200x1599, 1006x800
  and 1152x928.
  ENHANCEMENT 3 rescales the feature axes. It cannot change which blocks are
  identical - an exact duplicate still sits at distance exactly 0.0000 - but
  it does change the ORDERING of near-misses, which is the point.
  ENHANCEMENT 4's radius is calibrated on three photographs at one analysed
  scale. It is not a universal constant and is recorded as unsourced.
  ENHANCEMENT 5 can make the engine abstain on highly self-similar images
  that would previously have been scored. That is the intended behaviour: the
  guard fires when no usable match set exists, and a zero there would mean
  "no search ran", not "no duplicates found".

CHANGES TESTED AND REJECTED (full numbers in constants.REJECTED_ENHANCEMENTS)
  - Match radius 2.0. Chosen first on a sweep over 192x192 CROPS, where it
    gave zero false alarms and 49/49 precision. It does not survive
    matched-scale testing: on whole photographs it flags 0.3179 of campic and
    0.3354 of campic2 - a third of two AUTHENTIC images - because whole
    frames contain large smooth areas whose blur invariants are genuinely
    near-identical, which small textured crops do not. The crop experiment
    was not a valid proxy for whole-image behaviour.
  - A per-block texture floor excluding flat blocks from the match set
    (tested at LL variance 5.0 and 20.0). It does suppress those false alarms
    - authentic worst case falls from 0.3354 to 0.0211 at radius 2.0 - but it
    also removes the regions the forgeries were planted in, cutting detection
    from 3/3 to 1/3 on exact copies, which the shipped configuration already
    got right. Net loss.
  - Dropping the spurious mu_pq factor from Eq. 12's correction term. The
    printed formula B(p,q) = mu_pq - alpha*mu_pq*(1/mu_00)*SUM is
    dimensionally inconsistent: the correction carries an extra factor of
    intensity relative to mu_pq, whereas without that factor the term reduces
    to intensity x length^(p+q), matching mu_pq exactly. The SKILL already
    documents that this same equation suffered PDF-extraction damage
    elsewhere (the "mu_{t=2i,2i}" subscript). NOT APPLIED - it is a change to
    a printed formula, and testing showed it is not needed, since exact
    duplicates already sit at distance exactly 0.0000. Recorded so the
    observation is not lost.
