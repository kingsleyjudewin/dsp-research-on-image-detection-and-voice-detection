The three "False" rows in the before column are not abstentions. They are
CRASHES: campic and campic2 raised "Length of data must be even along the
transform axis" and gen.jpeg raised "start_level must be less than 1", each
after Pipeline C had already finished its work correctly. 485, 496 and 209
seconds of correct computation were thrown away by a failure in a pipeline
that, by the engine's own scope decision, cannot affect the score.

Read the rest honestly:
  - Both authentic photographs score exactly 0.000000 after, which is the
    right answer - neither contains a copy-move region.
  - Two of the four non-authentic images now score above zero (fake.jpeg
    0.003995 with 199 confirmed pairs, genratedimage 0.002272 with 48), and
    both real images score zero. That ordering is correct.
  - fake .jpeg went the WRONG way: 0.002343 -> 0.000000. The duplication it
    found at full resolution is below the size floor the analysed-resolution
    cap imposes. This is the cost of ENHANCEMENT 6, stated rather than hidden.
  - correct_verdicts is 0/6 both before and after, because the provisional
    sigmoid maps every observed raw_score into 0.3543-0.3654, which the
    harness bands as UNCERTAIN. The scalar carries the ordering; the
    calibration on top of it does not carry a decision.

PART 2 - GROUND TRUTH: WHAT THE ENGINE CAN AND CANNOT DETECT
================================================================================

A 64x64 region copied and pasted within a 256x256 crop of campic.jpeg, with
one operation applied to the pasted patch and nothing else changed.

  variant                       BEFORE raw  pairs    AFTER raw  pairs
  authentic (no paste)            0.000000      0     0.000000      0
  exact copy-move                 0.018326    117     0.018326    117
  paste + contrast x1.1           0.018326    117     0.018326    117
  paste + gaussian blur 3x3       0.000000      0     0.000000      0
  paste + gaussian noise s=2      0.000000      0     0.000000      0
  paste + brightness +8           0.000000      0     0.000000      0
  paste + JPEG q90 recode         0.000000      0     0.000000      0

On this particular crop nothing changed, and that is itself the finding: the
crop is a low-texture region (median LL block variance 2.82), and in smooth
content the blur invariants of a processed paste are not recoverable at any
radius that is also safe on authentic images.

On matched-scale ground truth - three whole photographs downscaled to a 384 px
long side, each with a 64x64 region copy-moved inside it - the picture is
different and the enhancement does pay:

  radius   AUTH worst    exact copy               blur3               jpeg q90
   0.05      0.0000     0.0256/0.0256/0.0256   0.0000/0.0000/0.0000   .../0.0017
   0.30      0.0000     0.0262/0.0271/0.0256   0.0000/0.0000/0.0013   .../0.0256
   0.50      0.0203     0.0280/0.0302/0.0256   0.0020/0.0017/0.0197   .../0.0256

At the shipped radius of 0.30 all three authentic photographs score exactly
0.000000 while a JPEG-q90 paste is recovered at 0.0256 and a blurred paste at
0.0013 - both of which the original configuration missed outright.

Why the original missed them: Eq. 27 turns an ABSOLUTE Euclidean distance into
a similarity, and the invariant population measured 2.19e-06 to 1.06e+16 with
typical inter-block distances of 5.2e+12 to 7.9e+12, against a T=0.95 match
radius of 0.0526 - a gap of 14.0 to 17.0 orders of magnitude. The effective
relative tolerance was about 1e-14, so only a bit-exact duplicate (distance
exactly 0.0000) or a pure multiplicative contrast change (which cancels
through Eq. 17's mu_00 division) could ever match.

================================================================================
PART 3 - WHY THE RESOLUTION CAP WAS NOT OPTIONAL
================================================================================

Once the feature space is standardised, a radius that detects anything real
explodes the candidate set. Measured at full resolution:

  image         blocks     candidate pairs at radius 0.30
  campic       459,225     11,842,381,512
  campic2      459,225     11,823,980,141
  fake .jpeg   273,965      2,298,223,816
  fake.jpeg    251,889        173,902,488

All four overflowed the 20,000,000-pair guard, and the run process was killed
outright by the operating system on gen.jpeg - this machine has 6 GB of RAM
and 11.8e9 pairs is 189 GB as a bare index array. Without ENHANCEMENT 6 the
engine could not score a single supplied photograph.

Choosing the cap:
  side   blocks   cand pairs   AUTH raw   FORGED raw
   256    9,153      688,960   0.000000     0.026221
   384   22,833    2,098,927   0.000000     0.010248
   512   42,657   71,262,223   OVERFLOW     OVERFLOW
384 is the largest long side at which all three authentic photographs still
score exactly 0.000000 and all three planted forgeries are still found.

================================================================================
PHASE 7.5 - ENHANCEMENT VERDICT
================================================================================

  Did enhancement improve separation?  PARTIALLY. Probability separation went
    from undefined (the real average could not be computed, both real images
    having crashed) to +0.005522, and the ordering is now correct: both
    authentic images at exactly 0.000000, two of four non-authentic above it.
    But +0.005522 is far below the 0.40 that counts as good discrimination,
    and raw separation actually fell slightly, 0.002664 -> 0.001998, because
    the resolution cap costs fake .jpeg the duplication it previously found.
    On ground truth the gain is unambiguous: JPEG-recoded and blurred pastes
    are detected where before they were missed outright.
  Did enhancement break anything?      NO. Four fixed contracts unchanged
    field-for-field, 3 EngineOutput construction sites x 11 kwargs,
    probability None iff not reliable across four input classes, deterministic
    across repeat runs, all methods within 40 lines, SKILL VERIFICATION blocks
    unchanged at 16 + 1. One behaviour deliberately changed: an image whose
    candidate search overflows now reports is_reliable=False rather than
    exhausting memory.
  Net result:                          BETTER. 3/6 -> 6/6 reliable, no
    crashes, 13x faster (338.5 s -> 25.9 s per image), and real detection
    capability added on ground truth.
  Final engine health:                 NEEDS MORE WORK - the detector works,
    but its calibration does not turn a working scalar into a decision, and
    the resolution cap puts a floor of roughly 130-250 original pixels on
    detectable forgery size.
