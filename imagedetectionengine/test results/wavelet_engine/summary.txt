WAVELET-DOMAIN FORGERY ENGINE - ONE-PAGE SUMMARY
Read this first next time you open wavelet_engine.

ENGINE            wavelet_domain_forgery   (skill_version 1.0.0)
SCORED PIPELINE   C only - Haar-DWT copy-move block matching. A (noise
                  residual) and B (compression history) run unscored, per the
                  SKILL: A's output "is not a scalar score on its own" and B is
                  documented 100%-defeatable by its own source paper.

OVERALL HEALTH    NEEDS INVESTIGATION BEFORE INTEGRATION
                  The detector genuinely works - 3/3 planted copy-moves found,
                  0/3 false alarms on the same photographs untouched - but its
                  calibration never leaves the UNCERTAIN band, and it can only
                  run at all on downscaled input.

SEPARATION SCORE
  before   probability separation: UNDEFINED. Three of six images CRASHED, so
           the real-image average could not be computed at all.
           raw separation: +0.002664   reliable 3/6   avg 338.5 s per image
  after    probability separation: +0.005522   (target >0.40; this is poor)
           raw separation: +0.001998   reliable 6/6   avg 25.9 s per image
  ground truth (3 photographs at the analysed cap, 64x64 region copy-moved):
           authentic 0.000000 / 0.000000 / 0.000000  - zero false alarms
           forged    0.010248 / 0.010248 / 0.010248  - 117 confirmed pairs each

TOP 3 STRENGTHS
  1. When it can run, it is exact and it does not false-alarm. A bit-exact
     duplicate sits at Euclidean distance EXACTLY 0.0000 in feature space
     while random block pairs sit at a median of 8.09e+11 - a separation ratio
     of 8.09e+41. On all three authentic photographs it returns 0.000000.
  2. The contrast invariance of Eq. 17 is real and measurable. A paste
     rescaled by x1.1 is detected with the identical 117 confirmed pairs as
     the untouched paste, because a multiplicative change cancels exactly
     through the mu_00 division.
  3. It localises. flagged_regions carries every confirmed pair's two block
     origins and the duplicate map is a genuine binary tamper mask, which the
     SKILL treats as Pipeline C's primary output rather than a by-product.

TOP 3 WEAKNESSES
  1. IT CANNOT RUN AT NATIVE RESOLUTION. Stride-1 tiling gives 459,225 blocks
     for a 1600x1200 image and 11,842,381,512 candidate pairs - 189 GB as a
     bare index array. All six corpus images overflowed the guard and the
     process was OOM-killed on gen.jpeg. Everything is now analysed at a 384 px
     long side, which puts a floor of roughly 130-250 original pixels on
     detectable forgery size, and cost fake .jpeg the duplication it had found.
  2. IT ONLY SURVIVES SOME OF THE PROCESSING IT CLAIMS TO. The SKILL's headline
     robustness is to "the noise/blur/contrast changes commonly introduced
     during the copy-paste process". Measured: exact copy DETECTED, contrast
     x1.1 DETECTED, JPEG q90 DETECTED after enhancement, blur 3x3 marginal
     (0.0013), gaussian noise sigma=2 MISSED, brightness +8 MISSED. Brightness
     is a genuine scope limit - the invariants are contrast-invariant, not
     DC-offset-invariant - but noise is squarely inside the SKILL's claim.
  3. THE CALIBRATION CARRIES NO DECISION. Every observed raw_score maps into
     probability 0.3543-0.3654, which reads as UNCERTAIN for all six images.
     0/6 correct verdicts before AND after, even though the underlying
     ordering is right (both real images at exactly zero, two of four
     non-real above it). The scalar is the SKILL's own engineering
     recommendation, not a corpus value, and it has never been calibrated.

BUGS FOUND (all fixed)
  1. THE ENGINE CRASHED ON HALF THE CORPUS. campic (1200x1599), campic2
     (1200x1599) and gen.jpeg (1006x800) raised out of pywt.swt2, which needs
     both dimensions divisible by 2**levels = 8. Fixed by symmetric padding -
     which the SKILL's Implementation Notes already prescribed and the engine
     had simply not implemented - with the residual cropped back to the
     caller's own geometry.
  2. A NON-SCORING PIPELINE COULD VOID THE WHOLE RESULT. Those crashes came
     from Pipeline A, which by the engine's own scope decision cannot affect
     raw_score, yet analyse()'s top-level except discarded a completed and
     correct Pipeline C result - 485 s, 496 s and 209 s of work. Auxiliary
     pipelines are now isolated and their failure is reported, not fatal.
  3. UNBOUNDED MEMORY IN THE CANDIDATE SEARCH. find_candidate_pairs built a
     Python set of tuples from cKDTree.query_pairs with no bound. The count is
     now taken with count_neighbors, which materialises nothing, and the
     engine reports is_reliable=False above the cap rather than dying.
  No contract violations. Deterministic across repeat runs.
  MINOR, NOT FIXED: constants.py's module docstring contains a text-corruption
  artifact - "not a rigorSKILL(Wavelet-Domain Forgery Detection)ously-derived
  optimum" - where a filename was pasted into a word. Cosmetic, in a comment,
  affects nothing.
  PRE-EXISTING, NOT FIXED: five 0.5 literals outside constants.py (computer.py
  lines 200/379/475, visualizer.py line 49). Identical in HEAD; out of scope.

FUSION LAYER WEIGHT TABLE
  +---------------------+-------------------+
  | Input condition     | Recommended weight|
  +---------------------+-------------------+
  | JPEG original       | LOW               |
  | JPEG recompressed   | LOW               |
  | PNG lossless        | MEDIUM            |
  | Resized image       | LOW               |
  | AI-generated        | ZERO              |
  | Low resolution      | MEDIUM            |
  +---------------------+-------------------+
  Copy-move detection is nearly format-agnostic - the duplicate is duplicated
  whatever the container - so the rows barely differ, and PNG edges ahead only
  because a JPEG recode of the pasted region is one of the perturbations that
  weakens the match. Low resolution is MEDIUM rather than LOW because this is
  the one engine in the system that PREFERS small inputs: it already downscales
  everything to 384 px, so a small image is analysed at full fidelity.
  AI-generated is ZERO on principle - a diffusion render contains no copy-move
  event, so a low score there is correct behaviour and carries no information
  about synthetic provenance.
  A LOW SCORE IS NEVER EVIDENCE OF AUTHENTICITY: the SKILL's own Key Findings
  state the compression-history detector is 100% defeatable by an adversary,
  and Pipeline C is explicitly blind to scaled or rotated copy-move regions.

CALIBRATION ASSESSMENT
  Status: NEEDS LABELLED DATASET. The empirical-CDF route is implemented and
  verified working. The provisional sigmoid (midpoint 0.05, slope 12) is
  anchored far above the observed operating range - measured raw scores run
  0.000000 to 0.003995 on the corpus and 0.0102 to 0.0271 on ground truth - so
  it compresses everything into 0.354-0.365 and never crosses a decision band.
  Action: supply >=10 authentic reference fraction-flagged scores, or re-anchor
  the sigmoid midpoint near 0.005 once a labelled set exists. Do not re-anchor
  it from the six corpus images; that would be fitting the boundary to the test.

FUSION ONE-LINER
  wavelet_domain_forgery: detects un-scaled, un-rotated copy-move duplication
  exactly and without false alarms when it can run (3/3 planted forgeries
  found, 0/3 false alarms, exact duplicates at Euclidean distance 0.0000
  against a random-pair median of 8.09e+11), and localises it to block pairs;
  but it must downscale every input to a 384 px long side to be computationally
  tractable at all - so it is blind to forgeries below roughly 130-250 original
  pixels - it misses noise-perturbed and brightness-shifted pastes, it is blind
  by construction to scaled or rotated copies, and its provisional calibration
  maps every real score into UNCERTAIN. Weight MEDIUM on PNG and low-resolution
  input, LOW on JPEG, ZERO on AI-generated, and never read a low score as
  evidence of authenticity.

PERFORMANCE
  338.5 s per image before, 25.9 s after - a 13x speed-up, entirely from the
  analysed-resolution cap. Slowest step is Pipeline C's per-block feature
  build: build_feature_vector was timed at 997 microseconds per block, and at
  native resolution that alone is 7.6 minutes for one 1600x1200 image.
  Memory is the binding constraint on this machine (6 GB total): the candidate
  pair set, not the feature matrix, is what overflows.

NEXT STEP THAT WOULD ACTUALLY MOVE THIS ENGINE
  Not tuning. Two things: (a) a labelled copy-move set to calibrate the
  fraction-flagged scalar, since the ordering is already correct and only the
  mapping is missing; and (b) an approximate-nearest-neighbour or
  lexicographic-sort matching strategy in place of the all-pairs KD-tree query,
  which is what forces the resolution cap and therefore the forgery-size floor.
