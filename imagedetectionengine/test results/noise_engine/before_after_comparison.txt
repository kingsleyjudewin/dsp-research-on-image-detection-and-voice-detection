Read that table honestly. The probability column did not improve - it went
from two confident wrong-in-principle votes and four gate-driven abstentions
to six deliberate abstentions. What changed is the raw score underneath: it
stopped being the constant 1.000000 and acquired 0.122 of dynamic range. On
THIS corpus that range still does not separate the classes; it ranks the two
"real" images ABOVE the two "fake" ones. That is reported, not hidden.

Why the before column shows four Nones: the pre-enhancement flat-content gate
misfired on campic, campic2, gen and genratedimage (62.0%, 62.0%, 61.3% and
55.4% of their blocks fell below a degeneracy floor set ten times too high).
So before the enhancement the engine voted on exactly the two images it should
have been most cautious about and abstained on the two authentic photographs.

Gate status after the fix, measured directly:
  image                saturated-px  sat gate   flat blocks  flat gate
  campic.jpeg                  0.0%      pass         15.7%       pass
  campic2.jpeg                 0.0%      pass         16.7%       pass
  fake .jpeg                  65.0%      FAIL          8.8%       pass
  fake.jpeg                    7.7%      pass          0.0%       pass
  gen.jpeg                     0.1%      pass         22.5%       pass
  genratedimage.jpeg           1.2%      pass         49.1%       pass
Five of six now abstain solely because no calibration was supplied. The sixth,
fake .jpeg, is additionally and correctly blocked by the saturation gate whose
verdict the engine used to throw away.

================================================================================
PART 2 - GROUND TRUTH: DOES THE SCORE RESPOND TO A REAL NOISE MANIPULATION?
================================================================================

campic.jpeg (camera) is the host. mobilepic.jpeg comes from a different
physical camera, so pasting a region of it into campic is a genuine
cross-sensor splice - what the SKILL names as the manipulation Pipeline B
"detects best" and that Pipeline A is the blind substitute for. Every edit was
verified to change only the pasted rectangle: 65,535 / 63,297 / 65,523 pixels
changed, 3.42% / 3.30% / 3.41% of the frame, ZERO changed pixels outside the
rectangle in all three cases.

  variant                              BEFORE raw   delta    AFTER raw   delta
  authentic host                         1.000000      -      0.046296      -
  cross-sensor splice 256x256            1.000000  +0.000000  0.120370  +0.074074
  region denoised 256x256                1.000000  +0.000000  0.037037  -0.009259
  region given foreign noise 256x256     1.000000  +0.000000  0.129630  +0.083333

Over 18 trials (6 paste locations x 3 manipulation types), measured through the
shipped engine:

  manipulation     BEFORE          AFTER
                   peak  inside    peak   inside   mean delta   delta>0
  splice           -     -         1/6    0.212    +0.04167     6/6
  denoise          -     -         3/6    0.273    +0.03395     5/6
  foreign-noise    -     -         2/6    0.318    +0.07716     6/6
  ALL              0/12  0.083     6/18   0.268    -            17/18
  chance rate            0.083            0.083

Before: the heatmap peak fell inside the known pasted region 0 times out of 12,
and the top-10% of blocks overlapped the paste 0.083 of the time against a
chance rate of 0.083. The evidence map carried no information about where the
manipulation was - it was exactly chance.

After: 6 of 18 peaks land inside the paste (4x chance), overlap runs at 0.268
against 0.083 chance (3.2x), and the global scalar moves in the correct
direction on 17 of 18 trials. The one trial that moves the wrong way is a
denoise at row 512 col 640 (-0.009259).

The ceiling on this is set by physics, not code: the corpus images are JPEG
QF74 and all nine share one byte-identical quantization table (luminance row 0
= 6,6,6,7,10,15,22,34), so they passed through a common re-encoder. The SKILL
states that Chen et al.'s own FRR tables degrade "by orders of magnitude" from
RAW to JPEG90 to JPEG75. Measured directly: the median per-block residual
variance of campic is 0.1358 against a 1-LSB quantization-error variance of
0.0833, so what survives in the residual is barely above the container's own
rounding error.

================================================================================
PART 3 - WHY EACH HALF OF THE FIX WAS NECESSARY (neither works alone)
================================================================================

Measured across the corpus and the 18 ground-truth trials:

  statistic          scalar                 corpus spread   splice/denoise/foreign
                                                            trials moving correctly
  var(W)   [old]     top-k% mean [old]         0.0000        0/6   0/6   0/6
  var(W)             flagged fraction          0.1255        5/6   2/6   3/6
  texture-conditioned top-k% mean [old]        0.1875        0/6   0/6   0/6
  texture-conditioned flagged fraction         0.1222        6/6   5/6   6/6   <- shipped

Conditioning on texture alone leaves the scalar pinned at 1.0. Changing the
scalar alone recovers dynamic range but only half the sensitivity, and it makes
denoising detection worse than chance. Both changes are load-bearing.

================================================================================
PART 4 - CALIBRATED ROUTE
================================================================================

The empirical-CDF route was never exercised by the corpus. Verified working
with 12 supplied authentic reference scores:

  campic.jpeg authentic          probability 0.4167   is_reliable True
  campic.jpeg + cross-sensor splice  probability 1.0000   is_reliable True

Before the enhancement both of those images had raw_score exactly 1.000000, so
any empirical CDF would have returned the SAME probability for the authentic
photograph and its spliced copy. That is the concrete difference the change
buys: a calibrated deployment can now tell those two apart, where previously it
provably could not.

================================================================================
PHASE 7.5 - ENHANCEMENT VERDICT
================================================================================

  Did enhancement improve separation?  ON GROUND TRUTH YES (0/18 -> 17/18
    correct-direction responses; localization 0/12 -> 6/18 peak hits, overlap
    0.083 -> 0.268 against a 0.083 chance rate).
    ON THE SUPPLIED CORPUS NO - raw separation went 0.000000 -> -0.032544,
    which is inverted. The corpus contains no verified splice and all nine
    files share one re-encoder, so it cannot measure this engine either way.
  Did enhancement break anything?      NO. Four fixed contracts unchanged
    field-for-field, 3 EngineOutput construction sites x 11 kwargs,
    probability None iff not reliable across every input class tested, all
    methods within 40 lines, SKILL VERIFICATION blocks 10 -> 12 (two added,
    none removed), no numeric literals outside constants.py.
  Net result:                          BETTER, with the separation claim
    restricted to ground truth.
  Final engine health:                 NEEDS MORE WORK - specifically, it
    needs a labelled calibration set. The mathematics now responds to the
    right thing; what is missing is any basis for a threshold.
