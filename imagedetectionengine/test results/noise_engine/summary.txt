NOISE-PATTERN (PRNU) ENGINE - ONE-PAGE SUMMARY
Read this first next time you open noise_engine.

ENGINE            noise_pattern_forgery   (skill_version 1.0.0)
SCORED PIPELINE   A only - blind local noise-level inconsistency. C runs
                  unscored; B and D are calibration-gated and never ran.

OVERALL HEALTH    NEEDS INVESTIGATION BEFORE INTEGRATION
                  Not because the mathematics is wrong - after the fixes it
                  demonstrably responds to real noise manipulations - but
                  because nothing anchors a threshold, and the supplied corpus
                  cannot measure it.

SEPARATION SCORE
  before   probability separation: undefined (4 of 6 images gated out; the two
           that voted both returned 0.999089)
           raw separation: 0.000000 - raw_score was exactly 1.000000 on all six
  after    probability separation: undefined (all six abstain by design)
           raw separation: -0.032544  (INVERTED on this corpus)
  ground truth, 18 trials on a real photograph with verified local edits:
           before 0/18 correct-direction responses, all deltas +0.000000
           after  17/18 correct-direction responses

TOP 3 STRENGTHS
  1. The core mechanism is sound and provably works when content is not a
     confound. On a flat synthetic field with uniform noise the engine scores
     0.0383; add one 256x256 patch at 3x noise and it scores 0.5924; make the
     same patch 0.2x noise (a denoised region) and it still scores 0.5924. It
     detects noise-level inconsistency in BOTH directions, which matters
     because the SKILL names denoising as the single largest failure mode.
  2. Honest scope discipline in the original build. Pipeline B's correlation
     predictor is deliberately not implemented because I_crit, tau and c are
     camera-specific and the SKILL says so; Pipeline D modulates confidence
     only, never raw_score, exactly as the SKILL instructs. Four audit tuples
     record every unsourced parameter and unimplemented module.
  3. After the fix the evidence map carries real localization information:
     heatmap peak inside a known pasted region 6/18 times and top-10% overlap
     0.268 against a 0.083 chance rate. Before, it was exactly chance.

TOP 3 WEAKNESSES
  1. The score has no absolute meaning. A real manipulation moves the scalar
     +0.034 to +0.077; six untampered images span 0.122. The between-image
     spread is larger than the manipulation effect, so no fixed cutoff works.
     This is why the engine now abstains without calibration.
  2. On this corpus it ranks the two authentic photographs ABOVE the two fake
     ones (raw separation -0.0325). The corpus cannot support the engine and
     the engine cannot score the corpus.
  3. The residual filter is not the one the SKILL names. F is instantiated as a
     single-level Haar LL reconstruction because the SKILL names "the Mihcak
     wavelet-based denoising filter" but prints no formula for it anywhere.
     That plain high-pass is the origin of the texture coupling that had to be
     regressed out downstream, and Lin et al.'s FDR enhancement is absent for
     the same reason.

BUGS FOUND (all fixed)
  1. raw_score was a constant. Exactly 1.000000 on all six corpus images, on
     all six global nuisance transforms of each, and on all 18 ground-truth
     manipulations - a cross-sensor splice, a denoised region and a foreign-
     noise region each moved it by +0.000000. Cause: 24%-42% of every image's
     heatmap cells were already clipped at the 1.0 ceiling, so the top-10%
     window lay entirely inside the saturated set.
  2. The flat-content gate misfired on ordinary photographs. Its degeneracy
     floor of 1.0 is about ten times the median per-block residual variance of
     a JPEG photo, so 62.0% / 62.0% / 61.3% / 55.4% of the blocks of campic,
     campic2, gen and genratedimage counted as "flat" and the engine abstained
     on four of six images including both authentic ones. Floor is now 1/12,
     the variance of 1-LSB quantization error; a constant-grey frame still
     trips the gate at 100%.
  3. The saturation check's verdict was computed and discarded. On fake .jpeg,
     65% of pixels sit at or above 250, the check returned failed, and the
     engine published FAKE at probability 0.999089 - on an image where the
     SKILL states the multiplicative PRNU term vanishes outright (Eq. 19).
     Only the confidence penalty was ever read; the boolean now gates the vote.
  No crashes, no exceptions, no non-finite values in any run.

FUSION LAYER WEIGHT TABLE
  +---------------------+-------------------+
  | Input condition     | Recommended weight|
  +---------------------+-------------------+
  | JPEG original       | LOW               |
  | JPEG recompressed   | ZERO              |
  | PNG lossless        | MEDIUM            |
  | Resized image       | ZERO              |
  | AI-generated        | ZERO              |
  | Low resolution      | ZERO              |
  +---------------------+-------------------+
  All of the above are ceilings that apply only once authentic_reference_scores
  have been supplied. Without calibration the engine abstains and its weight is
  moot. PNG earns the highest row because the measured damage here is
  compression: median per-block residual variance on campic is 0.1358 against a
  1-LSB quantization-error variance of 0.0833, so a JPEG QF74 residual is
  barely above its own container's rounding error. Resized is ZERO because
  resampling rewrites the noise field wholesale. AI-generated is ZERO on
  principle, not on measurement: a blind LOCAL-inconsistency test is
  structurally unable to flag a GLOBALLY synthetic image - if the whole frame
  lacks PRNU equally, every block agrees with its neighbours and the image
  looks maximally authentic.

CALIBRATION ASSESSMENT
  Status: NEEDS LABELLED DATASET. Not "broken" - there is nothing to break,
  because the SKILL states outright that Pipeline A's calibration is "not
  specified in corpus - engineering recommendation". The empirical-CDF route is
  implemented and verified working: with 12 supplied authentic reference scores
  campic returns 0.4167 and its spliced copy returns 1.0000. Before the
  enhancement both had raw_score exactly 1.000000, so that route could not have
  told them apart.
  Action: supply >= 10 authentic reference aggregate scores from the deployment
  camera. Until then the engine contributes raw_score, the heatmap and
  flagged_regions, and casts no vote.

FUSION ONE-LINER
  noise_pattern_forgery: detects local noise-level inconsistency well when
  content is not a confound (0.0383 authentic vs 0.5924 tampered on synthetic
  ground truth, and it catches denoised regions as readily as noisier ones),
  and localizes real cross-sensor splices at 3.2x chance; misses everything on
  heavily recompressed JPEG, is structurally blind to wholly synthetic images,
  and has no absolute threshold - a real manipulation moves it +0.034..+0.077
  while untampered images span 0.122 - so it ABSTAINS unless the orchestrator
  supplies authentic reference scores; weight LOW on PNG/high-quality JPEG when
  calibrated, ZERO on recompressed, resized, low-resolution or AI-generated
  input.

PERFORMANCE
  Harness average across the six images: 243.8 ms before, 189.7 ms after. Do
  not read a speed-up into that - the two runs differ by more than the code
  does. Timed properly on one image (campic.jpeg, 7 runs after warm-up) the
  spread is median 329.6 ms, min 290.3, max 491.3, so run-to-run variance on
  this machine is wider than the difference between the two harness averages.
  The honest statement is that the enhancement did not measurably change the
  cost. Slowest stage is Pipeline C's 64 per-cell FFTs together with the
  pure-Python neighbourhood-median loop in utils.grid_neighbourhood_median;
  ENHANCEMENT 1 adds one Laplacian per block and a single 1-D polyfit per
  image, neither of which is the bottleneck. No memory concern - the largest
  intermediate is one float64 copy of the image.

NEXT STEP THAT WOULD ACTUALLY MOVE THIS ENGINE
  Not more tuning. Either (a) a labelled calibration set from the deployment
  camera, which turns the working empirical-CDF route on, or (b) a real Mihcak
  filter - the SKILL names it as the shared residual-extraction step for
  Pipelines A, B and C and never prints its formula, and the plain Haar
  high-pass standing in for it is the origin of the texture coupling this
  engine now has to regress out after the fact.
