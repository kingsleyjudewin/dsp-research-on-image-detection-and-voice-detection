RISK CARRIED
  ENHANCEMENT 1 assumes the block texture / block noise relationship is
  monotone and roughly log-linear across one image. Where it is not - a frame
  containing both a dense synthetic pattern and open sky - the fit residual
  will misprice the extremes. Measured effect on the corpus: block statistic
  now spans 0.0278-0.1500 instead of a constant 1.0, and no image degenerated.
  ENHANCEMENT 2 changes what raw_score MEANS (a flagged fraction, not a mean
  anomaly level), so any downstream threshold tuned on the old scalar is
  invalidated. Since the old scalar was measured to be the constant 1.0, no
  such threshold could have existed.
  ENHANCEMENT 5 means the engine casts no vote in its default configuration.
  That is the intended outcome, not a side effect.

CHANGES TESTED AND REJECTED (full numbers in constants.REJECTED_ENHANCEMENTS)
  - MAD-sigma(W) as the block statistic: delta +0.00000 on all three real
    manipulations. Changing the statistic alone does not lift the saturation.
  - MAD-sigma(HH of W): degenerate on JPEG - 90 of campic's 108 blocks return
    exactly 0.0. An apparent +0.45455 splice delta was traced to disagreement
    between np.where(x>0,...) and np.maximum(x,eps) on values inside (0,eps),
    not to any detection.
  - MAD-sigma(HH of I), Donoho's estimator: raw 1.000000 everywhere.
  - Trimmed p10-p90 block variance: delta +0.00000 everywhere.
  - p5/p10/p25 of the block's 5x5 local-variance map (noise-floor estimator):
    genuinely reduced texture coupling, Spearman 0.9753 -> 0.7475 at p5, but
    localization stayed at chance (0.121 vs 0.111) and the scalar still read
    1.00000 on every image, authentic and manipulated alike.
  - SKILL Eq. 20's f_T as the conditioning feature: scored 0.076
    top-10%-inside on denoised regions, BELOW the 0.111 chance rate, because
    f_T is computed from the residual and so moves with the denoising in
    exactly the way that explains the signal away. The intensity-plane
    Laplacian scored 0.273 on the same trials.
  - Scalar = (top-k% mean - median) of the clipped heatmap: correct direction
    on 3/6 denoise trials against 5/6 for the flagged-block fraction.
  - Scalar = (top-k% - median)/robust spread of the unclipped field:
    saturates at 1.0000 on all seven images tested.
