================================================================
FOURIER / JPEG-GHOST ENGINE (fourier_jpeg_ghost) - DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY
Tested: 6 supplied images + 11 ground-truth cases built to the source
paper's own experimental setup. Generated 2026-08-19.
================================================================

OVERALL HEALTH:  NEEDS INVESTIGATION BEFORE INTEGRATION
   The engine was found INVERTED and has been substantially repaired, but it
   is not fixed on the supplied corpus and its recall is below the paper's.

SEPARATION
   Six supplied images : -0.5000 before, -0.5000 after. Unchanged, and the
                         sign is the point - the fake average is BELOW the
                         real average. This number is uninterpretable for
                         this engine: eight of the nine supplied files carry
                         byte-identical JPEG quantization tables, so the
                         "real" images are themselves re-encoded and cannot
                         serve as single-compression negative controls for a
                         double-compression detector.
   Ground truth        : 0 of 9 correct before, 6 of 9 after. Both authentic
                         images went from a confident FAKE (D_max 1.65 and
                         1.25) to exactly 0.0000, and detections moved from
                         q2=6 with texture-driven masks to the true quality
                         with masks of IoU 0.858 and 0.867.

TOP 3 STRENGTHS (evidence-based)
 1. The underlying mathematics is sound and the ghost is genuinely there.
    Thresholding the normalised difference map directly against the known
    paste gives AUC 0.9927 at q2=51 for a true q0 of 50, and 0.9876 at q2=71
    for a true q0 of 70. Eq. 10 with the true mask returns B = 1.19 and 1.04.
 2. Localisation is the best in this system, and it is corpus-backed rather
    than an engineering extension - the SKILL calls the segmentation mask
    "literally a segmented binary tamper mask". Measured mask IoU against a
    known paste: 0.858 and 0.867 at 512x384, still 0.820 at 1200x1600.
 3. It correctly declines the cases the SKILL says it cannot resolve. A
    genuine q0=95-into-q1=70 splice returns 0.0000, matching the SKILL's
    statement that the method "does not resolve the reverse case", and a
    quality gap of 2 also returns 0.0000.

TOP 3 WEAKNESSES (honest)
 1. STILL WRONG ON BOTH SUPPLIED REAL PHOTOGRAPHS. Both read FAKE at
    probability 1.0000 as reliable, before and after. The statistic roughly
    halved (0.6797 -> 0.4223 and 0.7271 -> 0.3386) and the winning quality
    left the degenerate low end of the sweep, but neither crossed Th = 0.19.
 2. RECALL BELOW THE PAPER'S CLAIM. Azarian-Pour report >95% sensitivity once
    the quality gap exceeds 22; this pipeline misses a gap of 30 (q0=60) and
    a gap of 10 (q0=80) entirely. A finer sweep finds both but false-alarms
    on both authentic images, so the conservative setting was kept.
 3. raw_score SATURATES AND CARRIES NO MAGNITUDE. Measured D_max runs 0.6 to
    1.8 against Th = 0.19, so every positive reports exactly 1.0. The engine
    is effectively binary. Left alone because clip(D_max/Th, 0, 1) is the
    SKILL's own recommendation and the corpus offers no alternative.

BUGS FOUND
  No crash, no contract violation. EngineOutput holds all 11 fields at every
  construction site and probability is None exactly when is_reliable is
  False, verified across four input classes including a null image.
  ONE REAL DEFECT, now fixed: the segmentation's two-class Otsu cut spent
  itself separating textured background rather than isolating the ghost,
  which left class-0 covering a median 0.607-0.781 of the frame on all six
  supplied images. 18-20 of every 20 candidates were then discarded by the
  area test as too large, so the ONLY candidates that ever survived were the
  degenerate ones at the extreme low-quality end - and the engine's winning
  q2 was 1 or 6 on all six supplied images and all nine ground-truth cases.
  NOTED, NOT FIXED: Pipeline A's EM loop hit its 30-iteration cap without
  converging on 3 of the 6 supplied images. Pipeline A does not score, so
  this changes no verdict; it is reported in every trace as em_converged.
  PRE-EXISTING, NOT FIXED: one numeric literal outside constants.py
  (condition.py, the 0.5 exponent in coverage ** 0.5). Present in HEAD before
  this work; out of scope.

CHANGES APPLIED (all evidence-cited; see enhancement_plan.txt)
  1. Three-class segmentation, lowest band is the ghost.
  2. Candidate must sit at an interior local minimum of its own q2 profile -
     implements a SKILL sentence the engine did not act on.
  3. Null ghost results vote at half confidence, because an authentic image
     and a reverse-direction splice both produce exactly D_max = 0.0000.

CHANGES TESTED AND REJECTED (do not re-propose)
  - Finer quality sweep (step 5 -> 1): detects 5 of 6 splices instead of 3,
    at the exact quality with IoU 0.923, but false-alarms on BOTH authentic
    images (0.6683, 0.5928) and on the reverse-direction case (0.7150).
    Scored over all nine with Th fixed: step 5 right 6 times, step 1 five.
  - Fewer grid shifts (step 2 -> 4): extra shifts never once improved a
    genuine detection - 1, 4 and 16 shifts gave identical D_max at identical
    q2 for all five detectable splices, every winner at shift (0,0) - but
    they do inflate the spurious maximum on undetectable images (0.7150 ->
    0.7150 -> 1.1457). Reverted anyway, because at the shipped quality step
    those cases already return 0.0000 and the SKILL sanctions this pruning
    only for a paste known to be block-aligned.
  - Segmenting a local-minimum DEPTH map instead of d: all nine still
    saturated, winning quality moved only 6 -> 16, mask IoU fell to 0.000.
  - Re-fitting Th away from 0.19: it was fitted at FP=FN on 2000 UCID images;
    nine self-built cases are not grounds to replace it.
  - Replacing raw_score's saturating clip: real problem, but the corpus
    offers no alternative normalisation to replace it with.

FUSION WEIGHT RECOMMENDATIONS
    JPEG, splice from LOWER-quality source, gap > 22 ......... MEDIUM
    JPEG, quality gap under 20 .............................. LOW
    JPEG, splice from HIGHER-quality source ................. ZERO (blind)
    PNG / any non-JPEG ...................................... ZERO (skips)
    Resized image ........................................... LOW
    AI-generated ............................................ ZERO (out of scope)
    Low resolution (below 16x16) ............................ ZERO (skips)
    A LOW score from this engine ............................ NEVER evidence
                                                              of authenticity
  MEDIUM rather than HIGH even in its best case: the engine still reads both
  supplied real photographs as FAKE at probability 1.0.

FUSION ONE-LINER
    fourier_jpeg_ghost: localises a splice better than anything else in this
    system - mask IoU 0.82-0.87 against a known paste, with the region's
    original quality recovered to within one sweep step - but only when the
    pasted content came from a LOWER-quality JPEG than the host was re-saved
    at, it is blind by construction to the reverse case and to quality gaps
    below about 20, its score saturates so every positive reads 1.0, and it
    still calls both supplied authentic photographs FAKE. Weight MEDIUM on
    JPEG input with a wide quality gap, ZERO on non-JPEG, AI-generated, or
    any use of a low score as evidence of authenticity.

PERFORMANCE
    94.3 s per image before, 84.2 s after, on 0.8-2.6 megapixel JPEGs - no
    regression. Dominated by Pipeline B's 320 recompress-and-segment
    combinations. Memory: the sweep holds 20 full-resolution float64 maps at
    once, about 300 MB for a 1200x1600 image; larger inputs will scale that
    linearly and should be watched.
