FILES AND METHODS CHANGED
  1. computer.py  GhostDetector._group_superpixels, new _lowest_band_threshold
     constants.py SEGMENTATION_CLASS_COUNT = 3
     Mathematically: the cut moves from argmax of two-class between-class
     variance to the lower of the two three-class multi-Otsu cuts.
     Risk: on a map that genuinely holds only two populations the lowest band
     is smaller than the true ghost. Mitigated by the existing minimum-area
     test, which rejects a sliver, and measured - IoU rose 0.320 -> 0.858.
  2. computer.py  GhostDetector._sweep_one_shift, new _normalised_maps and
     _is_quality_local_minimum
     constants.py REQUIRE_GHOST_LOCAL_MINIMUM, LOCAL_MINIMUM_NEIGHBOUR_OFFSET
     Mathematically: a candidate is admitted only if mean_{class0}(d at q) is
     strictly below its value at the two neighbouring swept qualities.
     Risk: recall loss where the minimum is shallow, and the sweep endpoints
     can no longer be candidates at all. Both are real; see the limitations.
  3. condition.py ConditionChecker.assess_segmentation_health
     constants.py NULL_GHOST_RESULT_CONFIDENCE = 0.5
     Risk: none to the contract. is_reliable is deliberately unchanged, so
     the vote still reaches the fusion layer, at a weight matching what it
     can actually distinguish.

CATEGORIES COVERED
  A (signal extraction) : #1 and #2 - the segmentation and the admission rule.
  B (condition checker) : #3.
  C (scoring)           : considered and REJECTED - see below.
  D (robustness)        : covered by testing at both UCID and corpus
                          resolution rather than by a code change.
  E (performance)       : NOT addressed. Runtime rose and is reported honestly
                          rather than traded against accuracy.

TESTED AND REVERTED (do not re-propose)
  - Finer quality sweep, DEFAULT_QUALITY_FACTOR_STEP 5 -> 1. It genuinely
    finds more forgeries: 5 of 6 ground-truth splices at the correct quality
    against 3 of 6, including the q0=60 and q0=80 pastes a step of 5 misses
    entirely, located precisely (q2=60 exactly, mask IoU 0.923). But it fires
    on BOTH authentic images, at B=0.6683 and B=0.5928, three times the
    threshold, and on the reverse-direction splice at 0.7150. Scored across
    all nine cases with Th held at 0.19: step 5 right 6 times, step 1 five,
    step 3 four, step 2 three. Finer sweeps give the maximisation more
    candidates among which to find a spurious minimum.
  - Fewer grid shifts, DEFAULT_GRID_SHIFT_STEP 2 -> 4. Extra shifts never
    once improved a genuine detection - 1, 4 and 16 shifts returned identical
    D_max at an identical q2 for all five detectable splices, every winner at
    shift (0,0), a deliberately misaligned paste included - and they inflate
    the spurious maximum on undetectable images (reverse splice 0.7150 ->
    0.7150 -> 1.1457). Reverted anyway: at the shipped quality step those
    non-detections are already 0.0000 at 16 shifts, and the SKILL sanctions
    this pruning only for a paste known to be block-aligned.
  - Segmenting a local-minimum DEPTH map instead of d. All nine cases still
    saturated, the winning quality moved only from q2=6 to q2=16, and mask
    IoU fell to 0.000. The q2 profile oscillates from quantization-table
    resonance at low quality far more strongly than any ghost dips.
  - Re-fitting BHATTACHARYYA_THRESHOLD off 0.19. It was fitted at FP=FN on
    2000 UCID images; nine self-built cases are not grounds to replace it,
    and doing so would fit the decision threshold to the test set.
  - Replacing raw_score's clip(D_max/Th, 0, 1). Measured D_max runs 0.6-1.8,
    three to ten times Th, so every positive reports exactly 1.0 and the
    fusion layer sees no magnitude. Real, reported as a limitation, but the
    normalisation is the SKILL's own recommendation and the corpus offers no
    alternative, so it is not replaced with an invented one.
