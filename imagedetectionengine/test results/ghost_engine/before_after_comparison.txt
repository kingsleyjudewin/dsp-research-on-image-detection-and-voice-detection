  WHY THIS TABLE CANNOT SETTLE ANYTHING, AND IT IS NOT THE ENGINE'S FAULT.
  Eight of the nine files in the two folders carry BYTE-IDENTICAL JPEG
  quantization tables (md5 49b048154bc1, luminance row 0 = 6,6,6,7,10,15,22,34),
  real and fake alike; only fake2.jpeg differs. Photographs from different
  cameras cannot share a quantization table. Every one of these files has
  therefore been re-encoded by one common pipeline on top of whatever
  compression it already carried, which means the "real" images are NOT
  single-compressed and cannot serve as negative controls for a
  double-compression detector. Whether the residual 0.34-0.42 on them is
  genuine double-quantization structure or leftover segmentation artefact is
  not determinable from these files. It is worth noting that the
  ground-truth authentic images, single-compressed by construction, return
  exactly 0.0000.

PART 2 - GROUND TRUTH (where the engine can actually be measured)
Built to Azarian-Pour's own setup: a 512x384 UCID-sized host with a 200x200
paste (20.3% of the frame), the pasted content given its own compression
history at q0 before being composited and re-saved at q1. Both quantities are
known exactly, so the true ghost quality AND the true tamper location are known.

  case                        before D_max   after D_max   q2 (true)   mask IoU
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  authentic q1=70                  1.6522        0.0000    - (-)         -
  authentic q1=90                  1.2527        0.0000    - (-)         -
  splice q0=50 -> q1=90 (dq=40)    1.2733        0.9684    51 (50)     0.858
  splice q0=50 misaligned          1.2836        1.0315    51 (50)     0.867
  splice q0=60 -> q1=90 (dq=30)    1.1766        0.0000    MISSED        -
  splice q0=70 -> q1=90 (dq=20)    1.1740        0.6126    71 (70)     0.628
  splice q0=80 -> q1=90 (dq=10)    1.2240        0.0000    MISSED        -
  splice q0=88 -> q1=90 (dq=2)     1.2013        0.0000    (SKILL: near-blind)
  splice q0=95 -> q1=70 REVERSE    1.3509        0.0000    (SKILL: blind)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  correct calls                       0/9           6/9

  BEFORE, the engine was not merely weak, it was inverted and saturated:
  every one of the nine returned D_max 1.17-1.65, three to nine times the
  threshold, EVERY winner sat at q2=6, and the authentic q1=70 image scored
  HIGHER (1.6522) than any genuine splice. Mask IoU hovered near 0.48 on the
  splices for the wrong reason - at extreme quality the recompression error
  tracks image content, so the segmenter was finding the pasted photograph's
  different texture, not its different compression history. The same 0.48-0.51
  IoU appeared on the reverse-direction case the SKILL says is unresolvable,
  which is the proof that it was texture and not ghost.

  AFTER, the two authentic images and the reverse-direction splice return
  exactly 0.0000, and the three detections land at the correct quality within
  one step of the sweep with masks that genuinely cover the paste.

  MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED - the ghost is really there and the maths really works.
  Segmenter-free check, thresholding the normalised difference map directly
  against the known paste: AUC peaks at 0.9927 at q2=51 for a true q0 of 50,
  and 0.9876 at q2=71 for a true q0 of 70. Eq. 10 evaluated with the TRUE mask
  gives B = 1.19 and 1.04 at those qualities. The signal was always present;
  the segmenter was the failure.

PART 3 - DOES IT HOLD AT THE RESOLUTION OF THE ACTUAL CORPUS?
The ground truth above is UCID-sized. The supplied photographs are eight times
larger, and SEGMENTATION_SUPERPIXEL_COUNT is a fixed 150 regardless, so each
superpixel covers eight times more area. The same q0=50-into-q1=90 case rebuilt
at 1200x1600 with a proportionally sized paste:

  fullres authentic q1=90     D_max 0.0000  prob 0.0  0 flagged regions
  fullres splice q0=50        D_max 0.7201  prob 1.0  mask IoU 0.8198

  The fix scales: the authentic image is still silent and the paste is still
  localised well. But the QUALITY ESTIMATE degrades - the winner sits at q2=76
  against a true q0 of 50, where the small-image version put it at 51. At this
  resolution the reported q2,m should not be trusted as the region's original
  quality even when the mask is right.

HONEST RESIDUAL DEFECTS
  - Recall is worse than the paper's. Azarian-Pour report >95% sensitivity
    once dq > 22; this pipeline misses dq=30 (q0=60) and dq=10 (q0=80)
    outright. A finer quality sweep recovers both - and false-alarms on both
    authentic images while doing so. That trade was measured and the
    conservative side chosen; see the rejected-changes list.
  - The two supplied real photographs still read FAKE at probability 1.0. The
    statistic halved but did not cross the threshold.
  - raw_score saturates. Measured D_max runs 0.6-1.8 against Th=0.19, so every
    positive reports exactly 1.0 and the fusion layer receives no magnitude.
  - Localisation is fragmented on the harder splice: dq=20 produced 5 regions
    for one paste, against 1 region at dq=40.
