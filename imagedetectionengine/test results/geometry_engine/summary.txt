================================================================
GEOMETRIC CONSISTENCY ENGINE - DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY
Tested: 6 supplied images + ground truth from an exact pinhole model
================================================================

OVERALL HEALTH:  NEEDS INVESTIGATION BEFORE INTEGRATION
   The mathematics is the most accurate of any engine tested in this
   system, and it is the only one that survives heavy JPEG. But it cannot
   run without caller-supplied object regions, and it abstained on all six
   supplied photographs.

SEPARATION
   Six supplied images : UNDEFINED before and after. All six abstain at
                         the vanishing-point gate (11-37% line consensus
                         against the 50% required). Ordinary photographs
                         do not contain the single dominant vanishing
                         structure Yao's method requires.
   Ground truth        : +0.9948 uncompressed, +0.9893 at QF30.
                         False alarms under 2px region error: 5/10 -> 2/10.

TOP 3 STRENGTHS (evidence-based)
 1. The geometry is excellent. Vanishing line recovered at 300.3-301.1
    against a true 300.0 (sub-pixel), and Eq. 7 recovers height ratios to
    about 1%: 1.4602 measured for a 1.45x forgery, 2.0087 for 2.0x.
 2. JPEG robustness is real and is this engine's unique value. Separation
    +0.9948 uncompressed and +0.9893 at QF30 - the regime where the
    Benford and CFA engines are already gated off entirely.
 3. Detection reaches a ~10-15% scale error (x1.15 scores 0.8371),
    consistent with the 6.7% threshold derivable from Eq. 8.

TOP 3 WEAKNESSES (honest)
 1. INERT ON REAL PHOTOGRAPHS. All six supplied images abstained, and the
    height-ratio stage never executed on any of them.
 2. CANNOT PROPOSE ITS OWN OBJECTS. With SLIC regions and an assumed ratio
    of 1.0 it scores raw 1.0000 on a scene that is authentic by
    construction - identical to the forged version - because 778 arbitrary
    superpixel pairs guarantee some pair looks impossible. The engine
    already gates this path correctly; the consequence is that it can only
    vote when a caller supplies object regions.
 3. FRAGILE TO REGION LOCALISATION. A 2-pixel error on box tops/bottoms
    still false-alarms on 2 of 10 authentic scenes (was 5 of 10); at 5px,
    8 of 10.

BUGS FOUND
  None. No crash, no contract violation, no non-determinism (the same
  image analysed 10 times gave identical verdicts - RANSAC is seeded). The
  behaviours above are design limits, not defects, and the engine's own
  notes already anticipated the alpha=1.0 problem.
  PRE-EXISTING, NOT FIXED: 3 numeric literals outside constants.py
  (utils.py:29, 284, 346). Present before this work; out of scope.

CHANGES TESTED AND REJECTED (do not re-propose)
  - Propagating region-localisation uncertainty into Eq. 8's sigma: false
    alarms moved only 3/8 -> 3/8 at 2px and 8/8 -> 6/8 at 5px, because the
    propagated term is small beside the paper's 0.1*alpha.
  - Relaxing the 0.5 inlier gate: brittle but corpus-mandated. A 1.1x
    forgery abstained at 0.4691 while 1.15x voted at 0.5358. Reported as a
    limitation rather than loosened.
  - Scoring the SLIC path: already gated, and testing confirmed the gate
    is right.

FUSION WEIGHT RECOMMENDATIONS
    Caller supplies object regions + expected ratios .... HIGH
    Caller supplies regions only (alpha assumed 1.0) .... MEDIUM
    No supplied regions (SLIC path) .................... ZERO (abstains)
    Heavy JPEG, QF30-95, with supplied regions ......... HIGH
    Resized image ...................................... MEDIUM
    AI-generated ....................................... ZERO (abstains)
    Scene without one dominant vanishing point ......... ZERO (abstains)

FUSION ONE-LINER
    geometry: catches scale/perspective-inconsistent splices with near
    perfect separation (+0.99) and, uniquely in this system, keeps that
    performance down to QF30 where every trace-based engine is already
    disabled; but it needs caller-supplied object regions, abstains on
    ordinary photographs lacking a single dominant vanishing point, and is
    blind to same-scale composites. Weight HIGH when regions are supplied
    at any JPEG quality, ZERO otherwise.

PERFORMANCE
    3.5-10.7 s per image on the supplied photographs, dominated by the
    O(n^2) SIFT recurrence clustering of pipeline A4.
