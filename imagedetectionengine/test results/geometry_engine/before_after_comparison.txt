PART 2 - GROUND TRUTH (where the engine can actually be measured)
Scene built from an exact pinhole model: horizon at row 300, five vertical
poles of equal real height at known depths. A forgery is one pole rendered
at the wrong size for its depth. Estimated v0 came back at 300.3-301.1
against a true 300.0, and Eq. 7 recovered ratios to about 1% (1.4602
measured for a 1.45x forgery, 2.0087 for 2.0x).

HONEST RESIDUAL DEFECTS
  - False alarms are reduced, not removed: 8 of 10 authentic scenes still
    false-alarm at 5px region-localisation error.
  - The 0.5 inlier gate is a hard cliff on a scene-sensitive quantity. A
    1.1x forgery measured 0.4691 and abstained entirely while its 1.15x
    neighbour measured 0.5358 and voted 0.8371. Not loosened, because the
    SKILL's abstain instruction is explicit.
