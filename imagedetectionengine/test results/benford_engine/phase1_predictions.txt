PHASE 1 - PRE-TEST EXPECTATIONS (written BEFORE the engine was run)
===================================================================

STEP 1.1 - CORE ENGINE CAPABILITY (extracted from the SKILL file)

Property measured:
  The leading-significant-digit distribution of QUANTIZED 8x8 block-DCT
  coefficients, and how far that distribution departs from the best-fitting
  member of the generalized Benford family
  p(d) = beta * log_b(1 + 1/(gamma + d^delta)).
  Raw score = max, over the sweep grid (base 10) x (zig-zag frequency 1..9) x
  (JPEG quality factor 80/85/90/95/100), of the UNAVERAGED symmetric KL
  divergence D_KL(p_hat||p_fit) + D_KL(p_fit||p_hat).

Signal present in FAKE, absent/weaker in REAL:
  A second quantization at a different step size, a contrast/gamma remap, or a
  GAN's feed-forward synthesis all disturb the coefficient-generation process,
  so the empirical digit histogram no longer lies on any generalized-Benford
  curve. Divergence rises.

Signal present in REAL:
  Thai et al. 2012 derive that natural camera DCT coefficients follow a
  Gamma-mixture (Bessel) law that is sharply peaked and heavy-tailed; such
  populations are spread across orders of magnitude and are therefore
  Benford-conforming. Divergence stays near 0.

Raw score meaning:
  0 = perfect conformance (natural). Unbounded above. No paper publishes any
  divergence magnitude, so no corpus-backed cutoff exists.

STEP 1.2 - PER-CASE PREDICTIONS

CASE 1 - REAL (campic.jpeg, campic2.jpeg)
  Expected probability : 0.0 - 0.30
  Expected raw_score   : low; the provisional sigmoid midpoint is 0.30, so
                         "low" means below ~0.30 for probability < 0.5
  Expected is_reliable : True. Both are JPEG with a readable quantization
                         table, so the block-transform premise is satisfied.
  Expected evidence    : histogram sitting close to its fitted curve
  Signal ABSENT        : no re-quantization / no remap
  Likely failure mode  : a camera JPEG at high quality has many near-zero
                         high-frequency coefficients; the max aggregation
                         picks the single worst grid cell, and the worst cell
                         is likely to be a high frequency with a thin sample.
                         A false alarm driven by counting noise is the risk.

CASE 2 - FAKE / MANIPULATED
  Expected probability : 0.60 - 1.0
  Expected raw_score   : above the real baseline
  Expected is_reliable : True if JPEG and QF > 50
  Signal PRESENT       : double quantization, splice mixing two coefficient
                         populations, or contrast enhancement
  Likely failure mode  : the SKILL contains an EXPLICIT negative result -
                         Singh 2015 shows first-digit statistics do NOT detect
                         copy-move / splice tampering; tampered and original
                         Benford curves are "visually near-identical". If
                         these images are local splices rather than global
                         re-encodes or remaps, the engine SHOULD miss them,
                         and that miss is documented behaviour, not a bug.

CASE 3 - AI-GENERATED
  Expected probability : uncertain, but leaning high
  Expected is_reliable : True if JPEG
  AI detection in SKILL scope: YES - explicitly the BEST-supported use case.
                         Bonettini et al. 2021 report 99.83% average accuracy
                         separating GAN images from natural ones, and it is
                         the primary pipeline this engine implements.
  Caveat that matters  : Bonettini's 99.83% comes from a RANDOM FOREST over a
                         concatenated feature vector of up to 540 divergences.
                         This engine has no classifier - it reduces the grid to
                         ONE scalar. The SKILL states a single-threshold rule
                         "would be expected to sit below the 3-feature RF
                         number" (which was >0.75). So expect degraded, not
                         99.83%, performance.
  Likely failure mode  : Bonettini's classifier "collapses to near-random
                         accuracy" when test images are JPEG-recompressed at
                         quality factors unseen in training. Every Case 3 image
                         here is a JPEG, i.e. recompressed after generation.
                         This is the single most likely cause of a miss.

CROSS-CUTTING PREDICTION
  The calibration is uncalibrated by construction (no reference scores are
  supplied), so every probability comes from the provisional sigmoid whose
  midpoint 0.30 and slope 10.0 are flagged UNSOURCED in constants.py and were
  set on SYNTHETIC imagery. Probabilities should therefore be read as ordering
  only. The quantity worth judging is the RAW SCORE separation.
