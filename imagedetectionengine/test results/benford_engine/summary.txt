TOP 3 STRENGTHS (evidence-based)
 1. Double JPEG compression is detected strongly and cleanly.
    Controlled test: single QF95 = 0.0906; QF30->95 = 3.6207 (40x),
    QF45->95 = 2.3682 (26x), QF60->95 = 1.6389 (18x). The enhancement
    IMPROVED this from 13x to 40x.
 2. No false alarms on clean single-compressed images: QF80/85/90/95/100
    all score 0.0764-0.0979, flat and low.
 3. The residual-lattice counter is a clean binary double-compression
    flag: 14/14/10 on double-compressed images, 0 on every
    single-compressed image tested.

TOP 3 WEAKNESSES (honest)
 1. NO discrimination on the supplied corpus. Real mean raw 0.1207,
    fake 0.0508, AI 0.0907 - reals score HIGHEST. 4 of 6 verdicts wrong.
 2. The statistic tracks image texture, not authenticity.
    corr(score, AC energy) = -0.76 to -0.89 across every configuration
    tested. Smooth heavily-quantized photos score high regardless of
    provenance.
 3. Contrast enhancement is not detected by a single scalar. Moin's own
    setup: gamma 0.5/0.9/1.5/2.0 at QF90 gave ratios 0.54x, 0.84x,
    0.85x, 0.40x versus unaltered - i.e. often LOWER.

WHY THIS CORPUS DEFEATS THE ENGINE
    8 of the 9 supplied images carry the IDENTICAL quantization table
    [6,6,6,7,6,7,8,8,7] - a flat custom table, not IJG-scaled
    (mean abs residual 11.047 against the nearest standard table).
    Real and fake alike passed through the same final encoder, so
    there is no compression-history difference between the classes -
    which is the one thing this engine measures well.

BUGS FOUND AND FIXED
 1. SELF-INFLICTED DOUBLE QUANTIZATION (severe). The SKILL's sweep
    quantizes at QF 80-100; on an image already saved at QF74 every one
    of those is a second, finer quantization. On campic.jpeg the applied
    step was exactly half the embedded step, forcing all coefficients
    even and putting 0.674 of the mass on digit 2 (Benford: 0.176).
    Score inflated 33x, from 0.1143 to 3.8317, on an AUTHENTIC photo.
 2. DEGENERATE CURVE FIT. The least-squares fit escaped to beta=3.56e16,
    gamma=4.07e16, delta=36.3, collapsing to ~1e-17 on digits 4-9 where
    real mass existed. 97% of the reported divergence came from those
    bins, at a magnitude set by log(1/1e-12) - a constant in the source
    file, not anything in the image. Fits are now beta 1.4-2.4,
    gamma 0.0, delta 1.5-3.0, in the region of Wang's published values.
 3. SATURATED CALIBRATION. 5 of 6 probabilities were exactly 1.0000,
    including both authentic photographs. Now spread 0.157-0.193 on the
    corpus and 0.18-0.999 across the double-compression sweep.

CHANGES TESTED AND REJECTED (do not re-propose)
  - hard chi-square gate on Moin's published range: this pipeline does
    not reproduce that scale (2x-41x off, varying with QF)
  - sublattice detection as the exclusion rule: misses non-integer step
    ratios (1.5, 1.75) that distort just as badly
  - bounding the fit exponent at Wang's 2.55: made separation worse
  - decades-of-magnitude gate: precondition was not actually violated
  - tightening the zero-rate gate: no measurable effect

FUSION ONE-LINER
    benford: detects double JPEG compression well (18-40x separation)
    and never false-alarms on clean single-compressed images; misses
    splices, contrast enhancement and AI generation entirely, and its
    score tracks image texture (r ~ -0.86) so it must not be read as a
    standalone verdict. Weight HIGH on suspected recompression, LOW on
    custom-quantization-table or single-compressed input, ZERO on
    never-JPEG input.
