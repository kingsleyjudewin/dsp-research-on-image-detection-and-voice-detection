  BEFORE, the harness probe recomputed Pipeline B with the quantization-step
  normalisation deliberately disabled and got a score IDENTICAL to the
  engine's own, to four decimal places, on all six images. That is the
  signature of the defect: A.3 returned QF=100, whose table is all ones, so
  the divide-by-q was a no-op and the engine was running in the configuration
  measured to separate in the WRONG direction. AFTER, the container's own
  quantization table drives the normalisation and the two no longer agree on
  any image.

  SEPARATION GOT NUMERICALLY WORSE, AND THAT IS NOT A REGRESSION.
  This engine detects DOUBLE COMPRESSION. Eight of the nine files in the two
  supplied folders carry byte-identical quantization tables, real and fake
  alike, so every one of them HAS been re-encoded by one common pipeline and
  every one is genuinely double-compressed. A working double-compression
  detector must fire on all six - which is exactly what it now does, raising
  every score by a factor of 2.0 to 2.5. The "real" label is not a
  single-compression label, so the fake-minus-real difference is not a
  measure of this engine's discrimination. It cannot be measured on this
  corpus at all, and the separation figure above should not be read as one.
  This was written down as the primary prediction in phase1_predictions.txt
  before the engine was run.

PART 2 - GROUND TRUTH (where the engine can actually be measured)
Sources synthesised so their compression history is known exactly. Three
families, all VERIFIABLY never JPEG-encoded (Pipeline A.1 gives s = 1.02 for
uniform noise, 1.02 for the 1/f field, and the Laplacian source is built
directly in the DCT domain and never encoded). Three seeds each.

  condition                          single   easy-double   separation
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  Laplacian AC statistics, norm ON   0.2446        0.2731       +0.0285
  Laplacian AC statistics, norm OFF  0.3084        0.2365       -0.0719
  1/f field                          0.2015        0.3458       +0.1442
  Laplacian ac90                     0.2778        0.2857       +0.0079
  Laplacian ac10                     0.0874        0.1425       +0.0550

  "easy-double" is the regime the SKILL says the method is strongest in:
  QF2 > QF1 with a non-integer step ratio (70->85, 60->90, 75->95).

  THE HARD CASES BEHAVE AS THE SKILL PREDICTS, ONLY WORSE.
  QF2 < QF1 (85->70, 90->60) gives separation -0.0780 on the Laplacian
  family and -0.0049 on 1/f. The SKILL calls this "the historically hardest
  case for every DQ-histogram method" and reports "substantially degraded
  accuracy"; measured, it is not merely degraded but INVERTED - a
  down-quality recompression scores LOWER than a single compression.

  QF1 == QF2 (85->85) reproduces the input file byte-for-byte, so its score
  is identical to single_q85 to four decimals. That is the SKILL's documented
  blind spot behaving exactly as documented.

PART 3 - PIPELINE A.3 IS SOUND; ITS CANDIDATE SET IS NOT
  On 20 ground-truth files encoded with standard tables, A.3 recovered the
  EXACT true final quality factor every single time - 85, 70, 90, 85, 90, 95,
  70, 60, 85, 100 across both source families - at exact-pixel-match rates of
  0.9968 to 1.0000. The SKILL's 94-99.96% benchmark is reproduced.
  On the six supplied photographs it returns 100 every time, because their
  encoder used a table outside the IJG family Eq. 26 generates. The method is
  right; the candidate set does not contain the answer.

  s over JPEGs of known quality, three source families:

  The classes OVERLAP: a real JPEG at QF98 scores below a never-compressed
  gradient. No threshold separates them, so the false positives on smooth
  content cannot be removed by moving the cut. The SKILL states the feature
  is reliable at "QF >= ~85"; measured, it works from QF50 to about QF90 and
  fails above QF95 - the opposite way round.

HONEST RESIDUAL DEFECTS
  - Separation on clean ground truth is +0.0285 in the engine's best regime.
    That is weak. The SKILL's own accuracy figures for this pipeline come
    from an SVM integrating all 10 frequencies, which is [ML - excluded]; the
    training-free peak-prominence substitute the SKILL recommends in its
    place is what was measured here.
  - The QF2 < QF1 regime is inverted, not merely weak.
  - The probability mapping is uncalibrated and its midpoint of 0.12 decides
    the verdict almost by itself: it sits above the pre-enhancement corpus
    range (0.03-0.09, everything read REAL) and below the ground-truth range
    (0.20-0.32, everything reads FAKE).
  - No spatial localisation. flagged_regions is always None, and evidence_map
    renders the 10 computed spectra rather than a claimed tamper map. This is
    correct per the SKILL, which calls block-wise re-application "not
    validated end-to-end in the corpus".
