================================================================
JPEG-COMPRESSION-ARTIFACT ENGINE (jpeg_compression_artifact)
DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY - generated 2026-08-19
Tested: 6 supplied images + 20 ground-truth files with known
compression history, across 3 never-compressed source families.
================================================================

OVERALL HEALTH:  NEEDS INVESTIGATION BEFORE INTEGRATION
   One real defect was found and fixed - the engine was silently running in
   a configuration measured to invert its own score, on every supplied image.
   Even repaired, its discrimination in its best regime is weak (+0.0285),
   and in the SKILL's documented hard regime it is inverted.

SEPARATION
   Six supplied images : +0.0115 before, -0.1224 after. NEITHER NUMBER MEANS
                         ANYTHING for this engine. Eight of the nine supplied
                         files carry byte-identical quantization tables, real
                         and fake alike, so every one is genuinely
                         double-compressed. A working double-compression
                         detector must fire on all of them, and after the fix
                         it does - every raw score rose by a factor of 2.0 to
                         2.5. The "real" label is not a single-compression
                         label. This was predicted in writing before the run.
   Ground truth        : +0.0285 in the SKILL's best regime (QF2 > QF1,
                         non-integer ratio) with the normalisation working,
                         against -0.0719 with it disabled - the state the
                         engine was actually in.

TOP 3 STRENGTHS (evidence-based)
 1. Pipeline A.3 is excellent and reproduces the paper's benchmark. On 20
    ground-truth files encoded with standard tables it recovered the EXACT
    true quality factor every time, at exact-pixel-match rates of 0.9968 to
    1.0000. The SKILL claims 94-99.96%; measured here it was 100% of 20.
 2. It correctly declines what the SKILL says it cannot do. A QF1=QF2 double
    compression reproduces the file byte-for-byte and scores identically to
    single compression - the documented blind spot, behaving as documented.
    After the fix, a genuine near-lossless image abstains outright rather
    than reporting a score its own model cannot support.
 3. It is cheap and fully deterministic: 1.7 s per image including A.3's 100
    JPEG round trips, and five repeat runs on one image gave byte-identical
    raw scores.

TOP 3 WEAKNESSES (honest)
 1. WAS RUNNING INVERTED ON EVERY SUPPLIED IMAGE, SILENTLY. A.3 returned
    QF=100 on all six, whose table is all ones, which made Pipeline B's
    quantization-step normalisation a no-op. Measured over three seeds, that
    configuration separates single from double compression at -0.0719 against
    +0.0285 with the normalisation in force. The sign flips. Nothing in the
    output said so; the probe caught it by recomputing Pipeline B with the
    normalisation deliberately disabled and getting an identical answer.
 2. THE A.1 GATE DOES NOT SEPARATE WHAT IT CLAIMS TO, AND CANNOT BE FIXED BY
    RETHRESHOLDING. It fires on never-compressed smooth content (s=27.8 on a
    gradient, 45.9 on a blob, against a threshold of 2.0) and fails on real
    JPEGs at high quality (s=1.0-7.3 at QF98-100). Those two ranges overlap,
    so no threshold separates them. The SKILL's stated reliable regime
    (QF>=85) is measured to be backwards: it works at QF50-90 and fails above
    QF95.
 3. WEAK EVEN WHEN WORKING, AND INVERTED IN THE HARD REGIME. Best-case
    separation is +0.0285. Where the image was re-saved at LOWER quality than
    its original, separation is -0.0780 - the SKILL calls this regime
    "substantially degraded"; measured, it is inverted, not just degraded.

BUGS FOUND
  ONE REAL DEFECT, now fixed: the silent normalisation no-op described above.
  It was not a crash and produced no warning - the engine reported
  is_reliable=True at confidence 0.967-0.984 on all six images while running
  in its inverted mode.
  NO contract violations. All four fixed shapes (EngineInput, EngineOutput,
  ImageMetadata, ConditionReport) are unchanged field-for-field; all three
  EngineOutput construction sites pass all 11 fields; probability is None
  exactly when is_reliable is False, verified across four input classes.
  Deterministic across repeat runs. No numeric literals outside constants.py.

CHANGES APPLIED (all evidence-cited; see enhancement_plan.txt)
  1. Prefer the container's own DQT quantization table over A.3's
     recompression search - the SKILL's own stated preference order.
  2. Abstain when the effective table is all unit steps, because Pipeline B
     is then measured to score in the wrong direction.
  3. Note the quality-factor regime in which the A.1 gate stops
     discriminating.

CHANGES TESTED AND REJECTED (do not re-propose)
  - Changing Pipeline B's aggregation. DC-only scores best on average
    (+0.1038 vs the shipped +0.0691) but inverts catastrophically on the 1/f
    family (-0.2913); mean-excluding-DC inverts on another (-0.0248). The
    shipped mean over all 10 frequencies is the only rule positive on every
    family tested.
  - Re-fitting the A.1 threshold: no separating value exists.
  - Gating A.1 on R2 share or AC energy: real JPEGs are MORE extreme than the
    false positives on both statistics.
  - Re-fitting the probability sigmoid: it would be fitting the decision
    boundary to my own ground truth.

FUSION WEIGHT RECOMMENDATIONS
    JPEG, spliced from LOWER-quality source, non-integer ratio ... LOW
    JPEG, spliced from HIGHER-quality source (QF2 < QF1) ......... ZERO
    JPEG, QF1 == QF2 ............................................ ZERO (blind)
    JPEG, integer quantization-step ratio ....................... ZERO (blind)
    JPEG re-saved near-lossless (most steps = 1) ................ ZERO (abstains)
    Non-aligned / grid-shifted splice ........................... ZERO (blind)
    PNG lossless / never-compressed ............................. ZERO
    Resized image ............................................... ZERO (the
                                block grid is destroyed; the SKILL states no
                                closed-form method in its corpus solves this)
    AI-generated ................................................ ZERO (out of scope)
    Low resolution (under 64 unsaturated blocks) ................ ZERO (skips)
    A LOW score from this engine ................................ NEVER evidence
                                                                  of authenticity
  LOW rather than MEDIUM even at its best: +0.0285 separation is a weak
  ordering signal, and the probability mapping on top of it is uncalibrated.

FUSION ONE-LINER
    jpeg_compression_artifact: recovers a JPEG's quantization table and
    quality factor essentially perfectly (exact QF on 20 of 20 ground-truth
    files) and detects globally double-compressed images, but its actual
    tampering score is weak (+0.0285 separation in its best regime), inverted
    where the image was re-saved at lower quality than its original, blind by
    construction to integer step ratios, to QF1=QF2, and to grid-misaligned
    splices, and it produces no spatial localisation at all. Weight LOW on
    JPEG input with a lower-quality spliced source, ZERO everywhere else, and
    never read a low score as evidence of authenticity.

PERFORMANCE
    1.68 s per image before, 1.72 s after - no regression. Dominated by
    Pipeline A.3's 100 JPEG encode/decode round trips, which the caller can
    switch off via CalibrationSettings.run_quality_factor_sweep. Memory: no
    concern; the block-DCT array is the largest object and is proportional to
    image size.
