FILES AND METHODS CHANGED
  1. contracts.py  CalibrationSettings gains container_quantization_table.
     engine.py     JpegEngine._quantization_table prefers it.
     constants.py  PREFER_CONTAINER_QUANTIZATION_TABLE.
     Only CalibrationSettings is extended; EngineInput, EngineOutput,
     ImageMetadata and ConditionReport are byte-for-byte unchanged, verified
     field-by-field by the audit.
     Risk: a caller supplying a wrong table would mis-normalise Pipeline B.
     Mitigated because the table comes from the file's own header, which is
     definitionally correct for that file.
  2. condition.py  new ConditionChecker.assess_quantization_table_usability.
     engine.py     wired into the post-computation check set.
     constants.py  MINIMUM_USABLE_QUANTIZATION_STEP,
                   MINIMUM_NON_UNIT_STEP_FRACTION.
     Risk: over-gating. It fires only when most steps are 1, which means a
     near-lossless image with no quantization structure to exploit - the one
     case where abstaining is right. Verified: a genuine QF100 image now
     returns raw 0.0000, probability None, is_reliable False.
  3. condition.py  new ConditionChecker.assess_history_feature_regime.
     constants.py  HISTORY_FEATURE_UNRELIABLE_ABOVE_QUALITY.
     Risk: none. It never changes is_reliable or any score, only the note.

CATEGORIES COVERED
  A (signal extraction) : #1 and #2 - both restore the integer coefficient
                          domain Eq. 4 is actually written over.
  B (condition checker) : #2 and #3.
  C (scoring)           : considered and REJECTED - see below.
  D (robustness)        : addressed by testing across three never-compressed
                          source families rather than by a code change.
  E (performance)       : not needed. 1.68 s/image before, 1.72 s after.

TESTED AND REJECTED (do not re-propose)
  - Changing Pipeline B's aggregation rule. Two alternatives were implemented
    and measured over three never-compressed source families, three seeds
    each. Mean-excluding-DC: average easy-case separation +0.0652 against the
    shipped +0.0691, and it INVERTS on one family (-0.0248). DC-only: much the
    best average (+0.1038 easy, +0.0716 hard) but it inverts catastrophically
    on the 1/f family (-0.2913), because DC prominence there saturates near
    0.93 under single compression and FALLS under double - the reverse of its
    behaviour on Laplacian-statistics sources. The shipped mean over all 10 is
    the only rule of the three that is positive on every family tested, so it
    stays unchanged.
  - Re-fitting DEFAULT_HISTORY_THRESHOLD to remove A.1's false positives on
    smooth content. NO SEPARATING VALUE EXISTS: real JPEGs at QF98-100 score
    s=1.0-7.3, below a never-compressed smooth gradient at 27.8 and a smooth
    blob at 45.9. Any threshold trades one error class for the other.
  - Gating A.1 on the R2 region's share of AC mass, or on AC energy. Neither
    separates. A genuine JPEG has an R2 share of 0.0009-0.008 against
    0.021-0.035 for the smooth never-compressed cases - the real JPEGs are
    MORE extreme on the very statistic that would have flagged the false
    positives. The fraction of AC coefficients below 1 overlaps too:
    0.947-0.972 for real JPEGs against 0.950-0.956 for the smooth fakes.
  - Re-fitting PROVISIONAL_SIGMOID_MIDPOINT. Its 0.12 sits below the observed
    operating range on ground truth (0.20-0.32), so every ground-truth image
    reads FAKE, and above the corpus range before enhancement (0.03-0.09), so
    every corpus image read REAL. The midpoint therefore decides the verdict
    almost by itself. It is left alone because the SKILL states calibration to
    [0,1] is "not specified in corpus" and the scorer already flags the route
    PROVISIONAL; refitting it on self-built ground truth would be fitting the
    decision boundary to the test set.
